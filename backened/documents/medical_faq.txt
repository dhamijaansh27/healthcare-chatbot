Diabetes is a chronic condition that affects how the body processes blood glucose.

Common symptoms of diabetes can include increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, fatigue and blurred vision. Symptoms can vary between individuals.

High blood pressure is a condition in which the force of blood against the walls of the arteries is consistently too high.

Common symptoms of high blood pressure may be absent, which is why it is sometimes called a silent condition. Regular blood pressure checks are important.

For medical emergencies, patients should contact emergency medical services or visit the nearest emergency department.
